PnP: Absolute Pose from 2D–3D Correspondences

This is how every new camera enters an existing 3D reconstruction. Once you already have a point cloud with known 3D coordinates — from triangulation (Part 7) or an earlier stage of a reconstruction — and a new photo that shows some of those same points, PnP (Perspective-n-Point) answers "where was this photo taken from?" It's the absolute-pose counterpart to Part 7's triangulation: there you knew two poses and solved for a point; here you know a cloud of points and solve for one pose. This single step — resection — is the registration engine inside every incremental structure-from-motion pipeline (Part 11).
